The Saxophone Skybar · channel tools

Private tools behind The Saxophone Skybar, a small YouTube music channel of sax house.

This page describes two small, private applications. They are used only by the channel's owner, on the channel's own Google accounts. They are not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to them.

Skybar Hub

Runs the channel's 24/7 live stream and reads its statistics. With the owner's permission it can read and manage the channel's YouTube live broadcasts and live chat (titles, thumbnails, replies from the stream's host bot), edit the descriptions of the channel's own videos, and read the channel's YouTube Analytics reports.

Skybar Outreach

Helps the owner write to small bars and guesthouses about playing the channel's music. With the owner's permission it can create and send emails from the channel's own Gmail accounts and read replies to them, so they can be answered and nobody is contacted twice.
