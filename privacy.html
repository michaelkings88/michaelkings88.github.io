Privacy policy

For the private channel tools of The Saxophone Skybar: "Skybar Hub" and "Skybar Outreach". Last updated 24 November 2026.

Who uses these tools

Only the owner of The Saxophone Skybar, on the channel's own Google accounts. The tools are not offered to anyone else.

What Google data they access, and why

  • Skybar Hub (YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API): the channel's live broadcasts and live chat, to run the 24/7 stream and its chat host; the channel's own videos, to edit their titles and descriptions; and the channel's analytics reports, to see what viewers watch.
  • Skybar Outreach (Gmail API): to create and send emails from the channel's own Gmail accounts, and to read replies to those emails so they can be answered and no one is contacted twice.

Where the data is kept

On the owner's own computer and a private server rented by the owner. Sign-in keys are stored there and are not shared. Live chat messages are processed to reply in the chat; the tools keep short logs and statistics for running the channel.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train general AI models. People reached by email can ask to be removed at any time and are never contacted again.

Limited use

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask about or delete any data these tools hold about you, email saxophone.skybar@gmail.com.
